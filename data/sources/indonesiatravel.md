Government tourism page, no author or date shown. A promotional source, so it is used for general points and not for history alone.

- Drums are wood with goat or buffalo skin, carried on the shoulders and struck with mallets.
- War rituals: sent soldiers off and welcomed them home. Later weddings, religious ceremonies and harvest festivals.
- Performers dance while playing, in processions through villages.
- Costume: colourful sarongs and songket headscarves.
- Bau Nyale festival and a Gendang Beleq festival.
- Where to see it: Sade and Ende tourism villages, and cultural centres in Mataram and Kuta.
