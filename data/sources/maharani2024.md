News feature, citing the Ministry of Education's Warisan Budaya site for the drum parts.

- Beleq is Sasak for large. Heritage of the Sasak people of Lombok, West Nusa Tenggara.
- Played for soldiers marching to war, to raise their courage, and to welcome heroes home.
- Drum parts: cowhide head, tap-wood body, leather rope and strap.
- A dozen or more players. Accompanies nyongkolan and other celebrations (hajatan).
