- Uses the spelling "Gendang Belek" in its title.
- Beleq is Sasak for big. Two drums: gendang mama (male) and gendang nina (female).
- Origins: accompanied warriors going to war; the sound was believed to make them braver.
- Now accompanies weddings (merariq, nyongkolan), circumcisions, a baby's first haircut (ngurisan or kurisan) and large ceremonies (begawe beleq), and appears at festivals.
- Can be performed static or in procession. Known in the Central, East and West Lombok regencies.
- Costume: full traditional Sasak dress; batik sapo' or sapuk (head cloth), dodot (belt) and bebet (waist cloth) are considered essential. Some groups now use their own uniforms, which some elders criticise.
