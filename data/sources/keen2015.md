Keen, P. (2015) 'Lampa Lampa Lombok, Pt. 3: The Sasak Marching Band of Gendang Beleq', Aural Archipelago, 29 July. Available at: https://auralarchipelago.com/auralarchipelago/gendangbeleq (Accessed: 31 October 2026).

Field recording blog, based on a visit to Lingsar, West Lombok.

- Means "big drum" in Sasak. Said to have started as a way to stir up warriors before battle.
- Two large drums, lanang and wadon, with goat-skin heads and interlocking patterns.
- Also ceng-ceng cymbals, reong gong-chimes, petuk, kempur, gong and suling (bamboo flute).
- Some ensembles have thirty or more musicians. The two large gongs need four men to carry.
- Nyongkolan: bride, groom and revelers parade through village streets. Street dangdut has become a cheaper alternative.
- Governments fund troupes and have them play at tourist festivals.
